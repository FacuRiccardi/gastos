Privacy policy

card-expenses · Last updated: November 3, 2026

What this app is

card-expenses is a personal tool for the owner and one other person authorized by the owner (a family member). It is used with Google accounts to keep track of credit card purchases.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission it has read-only access to Gmail (the gmail.readonly scope). It only looks at emails that carry a specific label, which a Gmail filter applies to purchase notifications sent by the owner's bank. It does not read, search or use any other email.

Signing in to the web app

People sign in to the web app with their Google account through Firebase Authentication, which processes the account name and email address. Only accounts authorized by the owner can read the data.

What data it stores

From each purchase notification it extracts and stores: the purchase date, the merchant name, the amount and currency, the last four digits of the card, the card type (credit or debit) and the first name of the card holder. In the web app the authorized people can also enter account names, assign each card to a person, and record each account's statement closing and due dates; this is stored too. This data is kept in a private database that can only be read by the owner and by the one other person the owner authorizes in the web app. The content of the emails themselves is not stored.

Sharing

The data is not sold, transferred or disclosed to anyone, except that the owner lets one other person (a family member) see the purchases in the web app. It is not used for advertising or to train any machine learning model. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the owner wants it. The owner can delete it at any time from the database, and can revoke the app's access to Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
